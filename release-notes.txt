Islander 0.9.1

Ask: up-to-date answers out of the box
- The assistant now looks things up before answering (web results, the latest news, Wikipedia and live crypto prices), so questions like "is GTA 6 out yet?" get today's answer, not one from the model's old memory.
- No API key needed. A key of your own in Settings → AI is now only a backup.
- It also knows your local date and time, so "tomorrow" and "tonight" are right.

New: send files to your phone from the Shelf
- Nearby phones and computers running the free LocalSend app (iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows, Linux) appear under the Shelf. Tap one to send everything on the Shelf, or open a file's actions and choose "Send to…".
- Phones can send to your PC too: the island asks first. Accepted files go to your Downloads folder and appear on the Shelf.
- If Windows Firewall stops phones from reaching your PC, the Shelf shows "Can't receive". Tap it once to allow phones on your network.
- Turn it off or change the name your phone sees in Settings → General → Nearby sharing.

Fixes
- Files deleted from your PC now disappear from the Shelf within a couple of seconds.
- A file's actions no longer spill out of the Shelf when phones are nearby.

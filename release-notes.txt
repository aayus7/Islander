Islander 0.9.4

Movies and shows
- Play a movie or an episode in mpv, VLC or Stremio and the island shows its real poster, whole instead of cropped into a square; the player grows to fit it.
- The title is the film's actual name instead of the release file name, and the line under it shows the director instead of "mpv". Episodes show their season, number and name (S1 E4 · Please, Carol).

Smoother and lighter
- The island responds faster: it opens sooner on hover, closes sooner when you leave, and its content settles without waiting.
- Lighter on your PC: while the island is tucked away it stops drawing, the music waveform stops analysing, and it checks the cursor less often when you're nowhere near it. Nothing it shows is any less live.
- Search or Ask is ready the very first time you open it (no plain first frame).
- On the left and right edges, the first hover right after moving the island works straight away.

Volume
- In fullscreen (games, videos), the volume keys show a small volume pill over the app without the island ever appearing.
- Windows' own volume pop-up is now kept away in more cases: with an admin app in front, after a hardware knob or headset button, or with vendor audio software.

Bluetooth devices
- The "Connected" pop-up opens with the battery already showing, instead of the name first and the charge a second later.
- AirPods Pro (1st gen): the animation's white background is gone.
- AirPods Max and Beats Fit Pro (and the other AirPods): no more purple edge around the animation.
- AirPods Max is no longer stretched sideways and sits snugly at the left of the pop-up.

Also
- Settings › About now says who made Islander, with a Ko-fi link if you'd like to support it.

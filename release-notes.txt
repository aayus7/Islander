Islander 0.9.1

New: screen recording
- Record your screen from Controls, iPhone-style: a 3, 2, 1 countdown, then a red rim around the island while it records.
- Hover the island to pause, resume or stop. The recording is saved to Videos > Screen Recordings; click the banner to show it.
- Small files in sharp quality (hardware H.264 at 60 fps) with your PC's sound.
- HDR screens are converted to normal colours, so recordings never look washed out.
- Settings > General > "Show in screen recordings" chooses whether the island appears in your videos.

Ask: up-to-date answers out of the box
- The assistant looks things up before answering (web results, news, Wikipedia, live crypto prices), so questions like "is GTA 6 out yet?" get today's answer.
- No API key needed. A key of your own in Settings > AI is now only a backup.
- It knows your local date and time, so "tomorrow" and "tonight" are right.
- The liquid glass bar now also shows up in screenshots and recordings.

New: send files to your phone from the Shelf
- Nearby phones and computers running the free LocalSend app (iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows, Linux) appear under the Shelf. Pick one and choose which files to send.
- Phones can send to your PC too: the island asks first. Accepted files go to your Downloads folder and appear on the Shelf.
- If Windows Firewall blocks phones, the Shelf shows "Can't receive". Tap it once to allow phones on your network.

Fixes
- Fixed a crash right after the AI answered a question.
- The AI bar no longer blinks black when it opens, and its shadow is soft like the other tabs.
- The island hides when a browser video (like YouTube) goes fullscreen.
- Photos dragged from a browser onto the Shelf no longer vanish after a second.
- Files deleted from your PC disappear from the Shelf, and a file's actions no longer spill out of it.
- "Show in folder" opens the right folder instead of Documents.

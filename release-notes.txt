Islander 0.9.2

Browser tabs in the island
- Islander Media Bridge is now on the Chrome Web Store (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera) and Firefox Add-ons. It shows what's playing in your browser tabs, with artwork, controls, live streams and downloads.
- The first time something plays in a browser without the extension, the island offers it once. Settings > About shows Add to Chrome / Add to Firefox, or Connected.
- Cancel a browser download straight from the island.

Updates
- Islander checks for updates every time it starts, including with Windows, and keeps trying until your connection is up.
- When an update is ready, the island asks each time you start Islander until you install it. Nothing installs without your click.

Polish and fixes
- New app icon.
- The shadow around the open island fades out evenly on every side (it was cut off in a straight line on the left).
- The collapsed music pill turns the album cover on track changes, like the open island.
- Hiding the island first tells you how to bring it back.
- The island's outline no longer skips a frame mid-animation.
- Bluetooth: friendlier device names, no duplicates, and more reliable connecting. Wi-Fi fixes.
- Smoother touchpad gestures and better placement on every screen edge.

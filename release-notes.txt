Islander 0.9.5

The cover card
- Click the cover in the music player and it grows into a big cover card: the art in high resolution, the title, a scrubber and play/pause/next/previous, all gliding in from the player. Click it (or press Esc) to go back; everything glides back into place.
- Sharp covers: Spotify and most players only give Windows a small copy, so Islander looks up the same cover at full size (Apple's iTunes and Deezer, used only when the artist and title match). Covers are remembered, so the next song from the same album, and anything you've played before, shows instantly, even after a restart.
- Movie and series posters in full resolution, from IMDb.
- The cover leans toward your cursor like an Apple TV poster, with a moving shine and a shadow that shifts with it. It eases back when you pause.
- A glow behind the cover pulses with the music, and the whole island fills with the cover's own colours, changing smoothly with each song.
- On a track change the big cover turns like the small one. Works on every edge of the screen.

Videos and movies
- YouTube and other videos show their thumbnail at its real wide shape, in the player and in the cover card, instead of a square crop.
- VLC: partial support. VLC doesn't report to Windows' media controls, so Islander reads its window: you get the title, the movie poster and play/pause and next/previous, but no scrubber.
- Browser extension 1.6: sends sharp, uncropped video thumbnails, and stays connected while a video is paused (Firefox used to drop it after a while, falling back to the browser logo and a blurry thumbnail). Update the extension to get these.

Also
- Islander has a tray icon: click it for Settings; its menu shows or hides the island, or quits.
- Switching between sources in the player is animated: the cover resizes and the title crossfades.
- Lighter: the new glows and colour background are drawn from tiny pre-blurred images, so the cover card costs about as much as the plain player.
- Stremio was listed as supported but never worked; it's no longer mentioned.

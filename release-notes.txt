Islander 0.9.3

Smoother
- A second live activity (a timer next to your music, a download, a second player) now grows out from behind the island into its own bubble and melts back in, with no seam. Its number and ring sharpen into view once it's free.
- In the right-hand corner of the screen the bubble buds off to the left, toward the screen.
- Picking up the island pops it up; letting go glides it into place in one motion, with no flash. It no longer springs open under the cursor right after you drop it.

On every edge
- On the left and right edges the island now has the same glowing, turning album cover as on the top, and shows a timer, download or second player as a bubble underneath.

New
- With the music player open, the volume keys add a volume row under it instead of switching away.
- Search or Ask: after an answer, press Backspace (or just start typing) for a fresh question, without clicking first.
- A new chat message (WhatsApp, Discord, Telegram…) replaces the banner on screen right away.

Fixes
- With music playing and a download running, hovering the island opens the music player again (it jumped to Downloads). Click the download bubble to see your downloads.
- Mister Download Manager: downloads no longer drop out of the island when MDM's window is minimized or it's slow to answer, and Islander reconnects if MDM switches its connection off.
- A timer or focus alert that finishes while Search or Ask is answering (or a panel is open) now always shows. Banners only count down while they're on screen.
- Bluetooth no longer gets stuck on "Unavailable" when Windows is slow to answer; the switch stays usable.
- Weather finds your town with Windows' location service, so it's no longer placed in your internet provider's city.
- Nearby sharing: the progress ring around a phone is no longer cut off.
- Timer presets no longer show a scrollbar on some setups.
